# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

ClubEngine is built by members of the KIU Game/Engine Development Club. Our goal is a well-designed, well-built engine, and we get there by holding our work to a high standard and learning from each other along the way. Anyone willing to put in the effort is welcome to contribute, whether it's their first commit or their hundredth.

We pledge to keep participation in this project and our club free from harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Caring about design: thinking through architecture, structure, and trade-offs before writing code
- Giving honest, specific code reviews that hold changes to the project's standards
- Keeping feedback about the code and the design, not the person
- Explaining the "why" when you request changes or propose a design
- Disagreeing openly and backing it up with reasoning, then committing to the decision once it's made
- Accepting feedback without taking it personally, and improving your work based on it
- Sharing what you know so the whole team gets better, and asking when you're unsure instead of guessing
- Crediting other people's work and ideas

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Insults, put-downs, or mocking someone's skill level ("how do you not know this?"). Pointing out a problem in code is fine; belittling the person who wrote it is not
- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or personal/political attacks
- Publishing someone's private information (contact details, address, etc.) without permission
- Taking credit for someone else's work
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a university or professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- This repository: issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and commit messages
- Club communication channels (chat servers, group chats) used for the project
- Club meetings, workshops, game jams, and events, in person or online
- Representing the project or club in public, such as at conferences or on social media

## Enforcement

Club maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate it.

Depending on how serious the behavior is, maintainers may take the following actions:

1. **Correction**: a private, written warning explaining what was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning**: a warning with consequences for continued behavior, such as no interaction with the people involved for a set period of time.
3. **Temporary ban**: a temporary ban from the repository and club channels.
4. **Permanent ban**: a permanent ban from the repository and club channels.

Severe cases (such as harassment or threats) may skip straight to a ban. Behavior that also breaks university rules may be reported to KIU.
